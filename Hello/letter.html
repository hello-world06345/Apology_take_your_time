Chapter I

The Letter

To: Tannuuu From: Yash July 2026

I've been sitting with this for a while now, trying to find the right words. But I think honesty matters more than perfection, so I'm just going to say it the way it lives inside me.

I hurt you. Not because you did anything wrong — but because I was breaking, and instead of letting you help me, I pushed you away. I took my frustration, my grief, and my confusion, and I aimed it at the one person who was standing closest to me. You.

When I said "maybe we should take a break so you don't get more hurt by me" — I know what that did. It wasn't protection. It was fear dressed up as consideration. And it made you feel like I was choosing to leave, when the truth is I was terrified of watching you get hurt by someone who loves you but doesn't know how to carry his own weight right now.

I lost someone I love. And in that grief, I became someone I don't recognize — frustrated, sharp, distant. My mind hasn't felt like my own. But none of that gives me the right to make you feel unsafe, unloved, or like you have to fight just to stay.

You stayed. Through every heavy silence. Through every time I was short with you. Through every moment where I made it harder than it needed to be. You stayed — not because you had to, but because you chose to. And I don't think I've ever told you how much that means to me.

So let me say it now:

Thank you for being the steady thing in the most unsteady time of my life.
I'm sorry for making you carry my pain when you were already carrying your own love for me.
I love you — not just when it's easy, but especially now, when it's hard.

I will choose you now and always, yesterday , today and tomorrow, day and night, everytime. Not out of guilt, not out of obligation — but because you are the person I want beside me while I learn to be better. I know I have work to do. I know trust is rebuilt slowly. But I'm not going anywhere.

I won't ask you to forget what I said. But I certainly hope I will be able to make things right with time.

With everything I have,

Your Yashuu
