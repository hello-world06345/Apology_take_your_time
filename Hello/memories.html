Chapter III

Our 3-Month Journey

Every moment here matters to me. Some are happy, some are heavy — but all of them are ours.

· The Beginning

The first time we really talked, and I knew something was different. You didn't feel like a stranger — you felt like someone I'd been waiting to meet without knowing it.

The Late-Night Calls

Those hours on the phone where we forgot about time. Your voice was the calmest place I knew, even before everything got heavy.

When You Made Me Laugh Again

After days of feeling numb, you said something so simple and I laughed for the first time in what felt like weeks. You didn't even know what you'd done.

When I Was Broken

I lost someone. And the world went quiet in the worst way. But you were there — not trying to fix me, just sitting with me in the dark. That's braver than anything I've ever done.

Growing Closer

Somewhere in the middle of all the chaos, we became more than just two people who liked each other. We became each other's safe place. I didn't deserve it, but you gave it to me anyway.

The Day You Stayed When I Pushed

I was cold. I was unfair. I said things I didn't mean. And you looked at me with those eyes and said, "I'm not going anywhere." I'll never forget that.

US

We're here. It's not perfect. I've made mistakes that still sit between us. But I'm writing this because I believe in what we have — and I'm willing to earn it back, one day at a time.

A Moment I Haven't Lived Yet

This one's for the future — the next photo we'll take, the next memory we'll make. I'm leaving this empty because I believe it's coming.
