Chapter II

You

The things about you I want you to know I see — even when I've been too blind to show it.

"The girl who stayed."

♡

You, when I'm broken

You didn't try to fix me. You didn't give me motivational speeches. You just sat with me — quietly, patiently — and let me fall apart without making me feel weak for it. That kind of love isn't common. I know that now.

◈

You, when you're tired but still stay

I know there were days you were exhausted — from your own life, your own battles — and still you showed up for me. You never made me feel like a burden, even when I was being one. That takes more strength than I've ever had.

✦

You, when you choose us

Every time I gave you a reason to leave, you chose to stay. Not out of weakness — out of love. You chose us, even when "us" wasn't giving you much back. I see that. I see you.

⚘

You, when you love quietly

Your love isn't loud or performative. It's in the small things — a "did you eat?" text, staying on the call even when we're both silent, remembering something I said weeks ago. That quiet love is the most powerful kind.

◦

You, just as you are

Not the version of you that tries to be strong for me. Not the version that hides her hurt. Just you — honest, real, sometimes scared, always enough. I want you to know: you don't have to earn your place here. You already have it.
